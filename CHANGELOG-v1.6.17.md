# Simple Syslog Server v1.6.17

## Traffic concentration alignment

- Traffic concentration now shows the same top-device set as the `Top devices` donut.
- Up to 10 devices are displayed, matching the existing backend Top 10 device statistics.
- Each slice represents that device's share of all messages in the last 24 hours.
- Tooltip and center values remain percentage-formatted with up to two decimal places.
