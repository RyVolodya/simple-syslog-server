# Simple Syslog Server v1.6.19

## Traffic concentration detail

- `Top devices` remains unchanged.
- `Traffic concentration` now shows each Top 10 device as its own slice.
- Each device slice uses its individual percentage of total 24-hour message traffic.
- `Other devices` is kept as a separate slice when devices outside the Top 10 exist.
- The center keeps showing the combined traffic share of the Top 10 devices.
- Percentage formatting remains limited to two decimal places.
